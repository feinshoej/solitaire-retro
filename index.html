Solitaire: Retro Edition

Classic Klondike Solitaire for iPhone, with Draw One and Draw Three.

How to play

  • Build the four foundations at the top from Ace to King, one suit each, to win.
  • In the seven columns, stack cards downward in alternating colors. Only a King can go in an empty column.
  • Tap a card to send it to the best place, or drag it (with the cards on top of it) where you want it.
  • Tap the deck to draw. When it runs out, tap the empty spot to turn the drawn cards over again.
  • Undo takes back moves. New Game deals again in Draw One or Draw Three.
  • Your game is saved when you leave the app, so you can pick it up later.

Settings and Game Center

The gear button opens Settings, where you can switch sound effects and haptics on or off. Sign in to Game Center to earn achievements, climb the best-time leaderboards and challenge friends. Game Center is optional.
